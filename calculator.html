What you are seeing

The calculation, not a black box.

For flat ΛCDM, the expansion rate is E(z) = √[Ωₘ(1+z)³ + ΩΛ]. The comoving distance is the numerical integral of c / [H₀E(z)] from 0 to z. D₍A₎ = D₍C₎/(1+z) and D₍L₎ = (1+z)D₍C₎.

For the volume, Vc(z) = (4π/3) fskyD₍C₎³. Every slider event recalculates in your browser and redraws the plot; no server is involved.

This teaching version ignores radiation and assumes spatial flatness. A future Astropy version could add named cosmologies, radiation, curvature and further distance measures.
